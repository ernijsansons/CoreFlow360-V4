## FAQ Section

### Headline
**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: How is CoreFlow360 different from other business management software?**
A: Traditional software requires you to input data and click buttons. CoreFlow360's AI agents actually do the work for you - they process invoices, follow up with customers, order inventory, etc. It's like having a full operations team, not just a tool.

**Q: Do I need coding skills?**
A: No! CoreFlow360 is designed for entrepreneurs, not developers. Everything is point-and-click. However, we do offer an API for custom integrations if you want.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
A: Most users are fully set up in under 30 minutes. Connect your first business (5 min), deploy your first agent (2 min), and you're running. Additional businesses take 5 minutes each.

**Q: What if I already use QuickBooks/Salesforce/Shopify?**
A: Great! CoreFlow360 integrates with 50+ popular business tools. Your AI agents will work alongside your existing systems, pulling data and automating workflows.

**Q: How secure is my data?**
A: Extremely. We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption, are SOC 2 Type II certified, and GDPR compliant. Your data is stored in multiple regions with automatic backups. We can't access your data even if we wanted to (zero-knowledge encryption).

**Q: Can AI agents make mistakes?**
A: AI agents are highly accurate (98%+ for most tasks), but they do ask for approval on important decisions (large invoices, unusual transactions, etc.). You set confidence thresholds - agents handle routine tasks automatically, escalate anything uncertain.

**Q: What happens if CoreFlow360 goes down?**
A: We have 99.99% uptime SLA with multi-region redundancy. If one region fails, traffic automatically routes to another. We've had zero data loss incidents in our history.

**Q: Can I try before I buy?**
A: Absolutely! Start with our free Starter plan (no credit card required). Or try Professional/Premium with our 14-day free trial.

**Q: How do I cancel?**
A: Cancel anytime with one click in your dashboard. No phone calls, no retention offers. Your data stays accessible for 30 days after cancellation.

**Q: Do you offer training?**
A: Yes! Free onboarding calls for all paid plans, video tutorials, documentation, and community forum. Premium plans get dedicated success managers.
