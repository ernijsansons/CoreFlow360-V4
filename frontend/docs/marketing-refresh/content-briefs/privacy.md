## Section 5: Your Rights and Choices

### GDPR Rights (EU Residents)
- **Access:** Request copy of your data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure:** Delete your data
- **Portability:** Export your data
- **Restriction:** Limit processing
- **Objection:** Opt-out of certain uses

### CCPA Rights (California Residents)
- **Know:** What personal information we collect
- **Delete:** Request deletion of personal information
- **Opt-Out:** Of sale (we don't sell data)
- **Non-Discrimination:** Equal service regardless of privacy choices

### Universal Rights
- **Update Information:** Through account settings
- **Export Data:** Download all your data anytime
- **Delete Account:** Remove all data permanently
- **Opt-Out:** Of marketing communications

## FAQ Section

**Q: Do you sell my data?**
A: Never. We make money from subscriptions, not from selling your data.

**Q: Can I delete all my data?**
A: Yes, anytime. Export it first, then delete everything permanently.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: Primarily in the US, with backups globally. All encrypted and secure.

**Q: Who can see my business data?**
A: Only you and users you authorize. Our support team can only access with your explicit permission.

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
A: You can export everything. We delete it 30 days after cancellation unless you request immediate deletion.
