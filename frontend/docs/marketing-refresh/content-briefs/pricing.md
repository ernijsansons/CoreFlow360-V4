## FAQs Section

### Headline
**"Everything You Need to Know"**

### Questions & Answers

#### Billing & Payment

**Q: Do I need a credit card to start?**
A: No, you can start your 14-day free trial without a credit card. We only ask for payment details when you're ready to subscribe.

**Q: Can I change plans anytime?**
A: Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any differences.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept all major credit cards, ACH transfers, and wire transfers for Enterprise accounts. Annual payments can also be made via invoice.

#### Features & Limits

**Q: What counts as an "AI action"?**
A: An AI action is any automated task performed by our AI agents, such as processing an invoice, sending a customer email, or updating inventory. Most businesses use 2,000-5,000 actions per month.

**Q: Can I add more businesses than my plan includes?**
A: Yes, you can add additional businesses to any plan for an extra monthly fee. See add-on pricing above.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my limits?**
A: We'll notify you when you're approaching limits. You can either upgrade your plan or purchase add-on packages. We never stop service unexpectedly.

#### Security & Data

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we're SOC 2 Type II certified, use end-to-end encryption, and maintain 99.99% uptime. Your data is backed up hourly across multiple regions.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, you can export all your data at any time in standard formats (CSV, JSON, PDF). There are no lock-ins or export fees.

**Q: Do you offer on-premise deployment?**
A: Yes, Enterprise plans can include on-premise or private cloud deployment options.
