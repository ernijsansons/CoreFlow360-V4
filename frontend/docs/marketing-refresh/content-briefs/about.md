## Our Story Section

### Section Headline
**"From Pain to Platform"**

### Timeline Format

#### 2019: The Breaking Point
**"Managing 5 businesses, working 100-hour weeks"**
Our founding team was collectively managing 12 businesses. Despite using 15+ different tools, we were drowning in operational tasks, missing opportunities, and burning out.

#### 2020: The Vision
**"What if AI could run the operations?"**
We realized the problem wasn't lack of tools - it was that tools required humans to operate them. We envisioned AI agents that could run businesses autonomously.

#### 2021: The Build
**"18 months of obsessive development"**
We assembled a team of AI researchers, serial entrepreneurs, and enterprise architects. No venture capital, no outside influence - just builders solving their own problem.

#### 2022: Early Success
**"First 100 customers in 30 days"**
We quietly launched to fellow entrepreneurs. Word spread organically. These early adopters saved 50+ hours per week immediately.

#### 2023: Rapid Scale
**"From 100 to 5,000 customers"**
Product-market fit was undeniable. We scaled our AI infrastructure, added enterprise features, and achieved SOC 2 certification.

#### 2024: Market Leadership
**"10,000+ entrepreneurs, $2.8B managed"**
Today, we're the leading AI-first platform for serial entrepreneurs, with customers ranging from solopreneurs to Fortune 500 companies.
