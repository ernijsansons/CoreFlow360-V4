## 12. Communication Best Practices

### Email Guidelines

**Always Include:**
- Clear subject line
- Personalization (first name minimum)
- Single clear CTA
- Value proposition (why should they care?)
- Easy opt-out option

**Never Do:**
- Send during incidents (unless incident-related)
- Over-email (max 2 marketing emails/month)
- Use clickbait subject lines
- Hide important info in fine print
- Send without testing links

**Tone & Voice:**
- Professional but friendly
- Helpful, not salesy
- Honest and transparent
- Respectful of time
- Celebrate their success

### Segmentation Strategy

**Segment by:**
- Plan tier (Free, Professional, Premium, Enterprise)
- Usage level (Active, At-risk, Churned)
- Features used
- Industry/business type
- Company size
- Engagement level

**Personalize:**
- Subject lines
- First paragraph
- Feature recommendations
- Use case examples
- Support resources
