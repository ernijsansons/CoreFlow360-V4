## Escalation Procedures

### Support Escalation Matrix

| Issue Type | First Response | Escalation 1 | Escalation 2 |
|------------|---------------|--------------|--------------|
| Technical Bug | Support (15 min) | Engineering (1 hr) | CTO (4 hrs) |
| Billing Issue | Support (30 min) | Finance (2 hrs) | CFO (4 hrs) |
| Feature Request | CSM (1 day) | Product (3 days) | CPO (1 week) |
| Complaint | CSM (1 hr) | CS Manager (4 hrs) | CEO (24 hrs) |
| Churn Risk | CSM (immediate) | CS Manager (1 hr) | Executive (4 hrs) |

### Save Attempt Process

**When customer wants to cancel:**

1. **Immediate Response (< 1 hour)**
   ```
   "I understand your concern. Can we have a quick call to ensure
   you get maximum value from your remaining time?"
   ```

2. **Discovery Call**
   - Understand real reason
   - Document specific issues
   - Offer immediate solutions

3. **Save Offers (in order):**
   - Free month to address concerns
   - 50% discount for 3 months
   - Downgrade to lower plan
   - Pause account for 3 months
   - Extended trial period

4. **If Leaving:**
   - Make it easy
   - Export all their data
   - Keep door open
   - Schedule follow-up in 3 months
