## Understanding AI Agents

### What Are AI Agents?

Think of AI agents as your tireless digital employees. Each agent specializes in a specific area and works 24/7 without supervision.

### Your AI Team:

**1. Finance Agent ("Finn")**
- Manages all accounting tasks
- Reconciles transactions automatically
- Generates financial reports
- Predicts cash flow issues

**2. CRM Agent ("Clara")**
- Nurtures leads automatically
- Schedules follow-ups
- Identifies upsell opportunities
- Maintains relationship history

**3. Inventory Agent ("Ivan")**
- Tracks stock levels
- Predicts demand
- Orders from suppliers
- Optimizes warehouse layout

**4. Compliance Agent ("Cole")**
- Monitors regulatory changes
- Ensures compliance
- Generates audit reports
- Manages documentation

**5. Growth Agent ("Grace")**
- Identifies market opportunities
- Analyzes competition
- Suggests strategic moves
- Predicts trends

### Controlling Your AI Agents

**Automation Levels:**

- **Conservative**: AI suggests, you approve
- **Balanced**: AI acts on routine tasks
- **Aggressive**: AI handles everything

**To Adjust:**
```
Settings → AI Configuration → Automation Level
Use the slider to set your comfort level
```
